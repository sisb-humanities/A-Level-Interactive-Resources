Activity 4 · Coursebook 1.4

Place each economy on the spectrum

No real economy is purely planned or purely market. Read the evidence for each of these ten countries, then place it on the five-point scale. An exact match scores 2; one band either side still scores 1 — there is no sharp line between systems, which is the point of the activity.

✓ All ten placed. Notice that even the two extremes — North Korea and Hong Kong SAR — are not absolute: North Korea allows some private trade, and Hong Kong's government still regulates and provides some public services. "Mixed" is a matter of degree, not a fourth category.

Sort ten organisations into their sector

Five Thai, five international. Ownership — not what an organisation does — decides its sector: a state-owned enterprise like EGAT can behave commercially, and a privately owned firm like the NHS's UK equivalent can be regulated tightly. Look at who owns it.
